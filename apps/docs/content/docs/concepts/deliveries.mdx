---
title: Deliveries, retries, and replay
description: Follow a webhook from queueing through its attempts and final outcome.
---

Each event creates one delivery per subscribed endpoint, and delivery is at
least once. By default a failing delivery gets up to eight attempts within 72
hours, with jittered backoff from about 15 seconds to 18 hours. Every attempt is
recorded, and a finished delivery can be replayed.

## Events and deliveries

An event records what happened. A delivery tracks sending that event to one
endpoint. Each delivery can have multiple HTTP attempts and replay cycles.

## Delivery states

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `pending` | Waiting for an eligible attempt |
| `leased` | A worker has claimed the delivery |
| `succeeded` | The receiver returned a successful response |
| `failed` | The delivery reached a terminal failure |
| `canceled` | Delivery was canceled |

Inspect the attempt history and `terminal_reason` when investigating a failure.
Attempts expose bounded metadata such as response status and duration; response
bodies and sensitive response headers are not included.

## Retries

Cascadehook retries eligible failures within the delivery's retry horizon.
The defaults are eight attempts per cycle, 30 seconds per attempt, and delays
from 15 seconds to 18 hours. The 72-hour retry horizon is fixed.

Each project has a delivery policy that an owner or admin can change in project
settings: 1–8 attempts, a timeout of 1–30 seconds, and non-decreasing retry
delays of up to 18 hours. A delivery cycle keeps the policy it started with, so
a change applies to new deliveries and to replays, not to cycles already in
progress.

Use `next_attempt_at` and `retry_deadline` from the delivery rather than
predicting the next attempt in your integration.

## Duplicate handling

Delivery is at least once. Your receiver may see a webhook again after a
timeout, an acknowledgement lost in transit, a retry, or manual replay.
Persist an idempotency record keyed by `Webhook-Id` with your business work.

## Manual replay

Completed successful or failed deliveries can be replayed when the endpoint
is enabled, verified, and has a signing secret. Replay creates a new cycle;
it keeps the same webhook identifier.

Supply the delivery's current cycle when requesting replay. If it changed
while you were reviewing the delivery, refresh before deciding to replay.
Replay can repeat downstream business work, so make that decision explicitly.
